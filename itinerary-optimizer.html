How It Works

1

Distance Matrix

Haversine formula builds an all-pairs distance matrix. Travel times scale per mode: walking 4.5 km/h, transit 22 km/h, taxi 35 km/h.

2

DBSCAN Clustering

Nearby attractions cluster into walkable neighbourhoods. Cluster radius adapts to travel style and pacing mode.

3

Pacing Modes

Compact — fewest days used.
Balanced — even spread across all days.
Relaxed — lighter days, more free time.
Intensive — max sights per day.

4

2-opt TSP Routing

Each day's tour starts nearest-neighbour then is improved via 2-opt edge swaps and Or-opt node relocation.

5

Constraint Scheduling

Opening hours, fixed-time reservations, and preferred time-of-day slots are enforced for every attraction.

6

Multi-objective Scoring

Six dimensions scored and weighted: distance efficiency, day balance, fatigue, cluster coherence, preference match, priority coverage. Weights adapt to pacing mode.
