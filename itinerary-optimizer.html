How It Works

1

Distance Matrix

Haversine formula computes all-pairs distances. Travel times are estimated per transport mode (walking 4.5 km/h, transit 22 km/h, taxi 35 km/h) with boarding overhead.

2

DBSCAN Clustering

Geographic clusters group nearby attractions. Cluster radius adapts to travel style and pacing mode. Cluster-coherent days minimize pointless cross-city movement.

3

Pacing Modes

Compact packs into fewest days. Balanced spreads evenly across all days. Relaxed gives lighter schedules. Intensive maximizes coverage per day.

4

2-opt TSP Routing

Within each day, a nearest-neighbour seed tour is improved by 2-opt edge swaps. Or-opt relocates single nodes and pairs for further gains on longer days.

5

Constraint Scheduling

Opening hours, fixed reservations, and preferred time-of-day slots are enforced. Attractions that can't fit are deferred or warned about.

6

Multi-objective Scoring

Weighted scoring covers distance efficiency, day balance, fatigue, cluster coherence, preference match, and priority coverage. Weights adapt dynamically to your pacing mode.
