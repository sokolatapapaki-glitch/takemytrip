# Project Tasks

## 1. Remove "Take the Kids" Component from Map Page

- On the Map page of the application, the "Take the Kids" component should not be displayed.
- This component is currently provided through the global `useContext`.
- It exists and is visible in all other pages.
- Exception: It must be excluded only from the Map page.

---

## 2. Navbar Button Positioning (Map & Cities)

- The "Map" and "Cities" buttons in the navbar should not be centered.
- They should be repositioned next to the "My Trips" button.
- The layout should be adjusted so that all three buttons are grouped together in the same section of the navbar.

---

## 3. Input Interaction Scroll Behavior (Home Page)

- When clicking the input component on the main (home) page:
  - The page scroll bar must NOT disappear.
  - The vertical scroll behavior of the page should remain unchanged.
- Currently, the scroll bar disappears when the input is focused, and this should be prevented.

---

## 4. Navbar Sticky Behavior

- The navbar must be sticky and remain visible at the top of the screen on all pages.
- When the user scrolls, the navbar should NOT move away or disappear.
- It must always stay visible regardless of page scroll position.

---

## 5. City Card Layout Adjustment (See Activities Button)

- In the City Card component (used in the City Page):
  - The "See Activities" button must always be positioned at the bottom of the card.
  - The button should maintain a consistent fixed distance from the bottom edge of the City Card.
  - This spacing must remain uniform across all City Cards, regardless of content height.
